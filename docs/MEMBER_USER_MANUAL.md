## 1. Accessing Your Account

### Web Portal
- Navigate to the Commandery portal URL (e.g. `https://app.ksji500.org`).
- Enter your registered email address and password.
- If accessing for the first time, click **Register**, fill in your details, select **Commandery #500**, and submit. The Commandery Registrar will verify and link your account to your master record.

### Mobile App (Android)
- Open the **Member Registration App** on your Android device.
- Log in with your email and password. Your device securely maintains your active session.
- If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on either platform to receive a secure reset link by email.

## 2. The Member Dashboard (`/me`)

Upon logging in, you land on your personal **Overview**:
- **Fraternal Title & Name**: Displays your full name and correct honorific (*Noble Brother* for N/B, or *Brother*).
- **Good Standing Indicator**: Shows whether your dues and welfare obligations meet the constitutionally required threshold for full fraternal privileges.
- **Cadet / Commandery Details**: Displays Commandery #500, baptismal records, and active rank.
- **Birthdays Widget**: Highlights fellow brothers celebrating birthdays in the current month.
- **Quick Action Bar**: One-click navigation to your ID card, payment receipt submission, dues ledger, and personal reports.

## 3. Digital Membership ID Card

Your digital ID card serves as an official credential for fraternal verification and meetings.

1. Navigate to **Digital ID Card** (`/me/id-card` on web or via mobile menu).
2. The card displays:
   - Your official portrait photograph.
   - Rank, title, and current Commandery affiliation.
   - Commandery #500 official crest and seal.
   - A unique, cryptographically verifiable **QR Code**.
3. **Verification**: Officers can scan your QR code with the mobile scanner to confirm active membership and good standing in real time.
4. **Export**: Click **Download / Print ID Card** to generate an offline PDF copy.

## 4. Maintaining Your Service Dossier

Your service dossier preserves your history in the Order. You can view and update each section directly:

- **Main Bio (`/me/edit`)**: Personal contact info, residential and postal addresses, profession, and hometown.
- **Exemplification (`/me/education`)**: Your progression through the Degrees of the Order:
  - *1st Degree*
  - *2nd & 3rd Degree*
  - *4th Degree (Chevaliers)*
  - *Noble Degree (Nobles Temple)*
- **Uniform & Military Ranks (`/me/military`)**: Record of uniform blessing, commission dates, and active ranks.
- **Positions Held (`/me/positions`)**: Past and current elected or appointed offices at Local, Battalion, District, Regimental, Grand, or Supreme levels.
- **Family & Emergency Contacts (`/me/family`, `/me/emergency`)**: Spouse, children, dependents, and emergency points of contact.

> *Note: Updates to critical identity fields (e.g. date of birth, name changes) are submitted to the Registrar for audit verification before being finalized in the master roll.*

## 5. Financial Ledger & Dues Tracking

Navigate to **Financials & Dues** (`/me/financials`) to view your real-time financial standing:

### Ledger Components
- **Arrears Brought Forward**: Any unpaid balance rolled over from prior years.
- **Annual Assessment**: The dues assessment for the active calendar year (reflecting applicable age-based discounts).
- **Total Obligation**: The sum of prior arrears and the current year's assessment.
- **Total Paid**: All payments officially verified and recorded by the Financial Registrar.
- **Outstanding Balance**: The remaining amount due. A balance of `GH¢ 0.00` indicates your account is cleared.

### Payment Timeline & Benchmarks
- **1st Half Benchmark (by August 31)**: Requires payment of all prior arrears plus at least 50% of the current year's assessment to maintain good standing.
- **2nd Half Benchmark (by September 1)**: Requires 100% full settlement of all obligations.

## 6. Submitting MoMo Payment Receipts

When you make a payment via Mobile Money (MoMo) or bank transfer, you can upload your confirmation receipt directly to expedite reconciliation:

1. Navigate to **Submit MoMo Receipt** (`/me/payments/upload`).
2. Fill in the submission form:
   - **Amount Paid (GH¢)**: Enter the exact amount sent.
   - **Payment Date**: The date the transaction was completed.
   - **Transaction ID / Reference**: The SMS confirmation reference code from MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or your bank.
   - **Payment Type**: Select *Annual Assessment / Dues* or *Welfare Contribution*.
   - **Receipt Screenshot**: Attach a screenshot of your MoMo SMS or payment slip.
3. Click **Submit Receipt**.
4. Your receipt enters the Financial Registrar's verification queue. Once approved, your ledger updates automatically.

## 7. Welfare Scheme Overview

Navigate to **Welfare Scheme** (`/me/welfare`) to review your participation in the Commandery Welfare Scheme:
- **Monthly Welfare Rate**: Standard monthly welfare dues (e.g. `GH¢ 25.00/month`).
- **Cumulative Contributions**: Total contributions credited to your welfare ledger from your effective start date (joined date or ledger inception).
- **Senior 80+ Exemption**: Brothers who attain age 80 are automatically recognized as Senior Exempt, with expected contributions waived in gratitude for lifetime service.
- **Benefit Entitlements**: Review welfare provisions (bereavement support, medical solidarity, and fraternal benefits) according to Commandery bylaws.

## 8. Personal Standing Report

Need an official statement for Battalion, District, or Grand Commandery functions?

1. Navigate to **Personal Report** (`/me/report`).
2. The system generates a comprehensive **Fraternal & Financial Standing Statement** detailing:
   - Your current Good Standing certification.
   - Assessment and payment breakdown for the year.
   - Welfare scheme compliance status.
   - Official Commandery #500 seal and authentication timestamp.
3. Click **Print / Save as PDF** to generate an official document suitable for submission to higher echelons or transferring commanderies.

## 9. Meeting Attendance & Check-In

Commandery #500 tracks attendance to ensure constitutional participation:

### GPS Geofenced Check-In (Mobile App)
1. When you arrive at the meeting venue (within the designated perimeter), open the mobile app.
2. Tap **Attendance Check-In**.
3. Allow location permissions when prompted. The app verifies your presence within the venue geofence and registers you as **Present (GPS)**.

### QR Code Check-In
1. If GPS is unavailable or indoors, present your **Digital ID Card** to the Registrar at the entrance.
2. The officer scans your QR code to immediately mark you as **Present (QR Scan)**.

### Submitting an Excuse for Absence
If you are unable to attend a meeting due to illness, official duty, or bereavement:
1. Navigate to **Attendance** (`/me/attendance`).
2. Select the upcoming meeting and click **Submit Excuse**.
3. Provide your reason and submit. The Registrar will review and update your status to **Excused** upon approval.
